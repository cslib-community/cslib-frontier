# Roadmap: Communication, queries, learning and other models

Part of the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md), which explains what an entry is and when one is added or removed.

- **Lifting** ([#890](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/890)): applications of deterministic lifting, such as partition-number separations, and randomized lifting.
  Also round-sensitive refinements and improved explicit constants for low-discrepancy deterministic simulation.
- **Randomized query complexity of game trees**: for the AND–OR (NAND) tree on `n = 4 ^ k` leaves, the Saks–Wigderson lower bound `R₀ = Ω(n ^ (log₂ α))`, `α = (1 + √33) / 4`, matching Snir's upper bound.
  Also bounded-error bounds for the same trees, and Saks–Wigderson's results for general read-once formulas and MIN/MAX game trees over integer leaves.
- **Number-on-forehead multiparty protocols**: a sharper `Ω(n / 2 ^ k)` bound (source unverified) for generalized inner product, Raz's matrix multiplication and NOF disjointness bounds, and `ACC⁰` and branching-program applications.
  A two-party companion is the Aho–Ullman–Yannakakis bound `D(f) = O(N⁰(f) N¹(f))`.
- **Exactly-N in the number-on-forehead model**: the converse of the Chandra–Furst–Lipton lower bound from corner-free colorings, upper bounds from Behrend's construction, quantitative corners bounds, and the `k`-player version.
- **Information complexity**: distributional-error information complexity; additivity and the Braverman–Rao direct sum `IC_{μⁿ}(fⁿ) = n · IC_μ(f)`, with private sampling from conditional marginals; information equals amortized communication.
  Also the Bar-Yossef–Jayram–Kumar–Sivakumar `Ω(n)` disjointness bound via the information cost of AND, with conditional information cost under a non-product `μ`.
- **Number-in-hand protocols**: multiparty number-in-hand protocols, direct-product results, and reductions to streaming, sketching and cell-probe lower bounds.
- **Sign-rank and unbounded error** ([#3262](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3262)): Forster's exact isotropic-position lemma, the Alon–Frankl–Rödl counting bound, and the Razborov–Sherstov exponential sign-rank bound for an `AC⁰` function, separating `Σ₂^cc` from `UPP^cc`.
- **Dynamic cell-probe lower bounds**: the Fredman–Saks bound `t_q = Ω(log n / log(t_u (w + 1)))` for dynamic prefix parity with amortized updates and with Las Vegas randomization, through cell-probe Yao averaging.
  Also Pătraşcu–Demaine's trade-off `t_q lg(t_u / t_q) = Ω(lg n)` and reductions to dynamic connectivity.
- **Streaming and sketching**: native time and space bounds for Count-Min with hash-seed storage and evaluation; the AMS sketch for `F₀`, the AMS `F_k` estimator for streams of unknown length, and the `n^{Ω(1)}` lower bounds for `F_k` with `k ≥ 6`; `ProbComp` samplers and native realizations of the AMS sketches and CountSketch.
  Also turnstile updates as stream items, merging of Misra–Gries summaries, heavy-hitter recovery over the whole universe (dyadic or group-testing schemes) and heavy-hitter space lower bounds.
- **Join-size sketches**: the Alon–Gibbons–Matias–Szegedy tug-of-war join signature over turnstile streams with insertions and deletions, and their signature-size lower bound for join sizes (Theorem 4.3).
- **SQL query semantics** ([#5282](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/5282)): the executable SQL semantics adapted from Evelyn Mitchell's `sql_equiv`, extended with subqueries (`IN`, `EXISTS`, scalar and correlated), outer joins, grouping and aggregates, `ORDER BY`, `LIMIT` and `OFFSET`, window functions, recursive queries and updates.
  Also decimals and casts, bounded integers, collations, dialect parameters, and a surface-SQL parser with a verified name-resolution pass.
- **Distinct elements**: the space-optimal `F₀` estimators (Bar-Yossef et al. Theorems 2–3, Kane–Nelson–Woodruff `O(ε⁻² + log n)`) and the `Ω(ε⁻²)` and `Ω(log n)` lower bounds for approximate `F₀`.
- **Locality-sensitive hashing** ([#3361](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3361)): the `(c, r)`-ANN structure realized on the word RAM with hashed buckets, seed storage and native `n^ρ` query and preprocessing time.
  Also `p ∈ (0, 2) \ {1, 2}` `p`-stable LSH, smaller min-wise families, and LSH lower bounds (O'Donnell–Wu–Zhou, source unverified).
- **Dimension reduction**: the Johnson–Lindenstrauss lemma for Achlioptas's sparse `{-√3, 0, √3}` distribution, Gaussian and spherical projections, and fast and sparse JL transforms; the Larsen–Nelson lower bound `k = Ω(ε⁻² log n)`; and JL maps as streaming sketches with `ProbComp` samplers and native costs for applying the projection.
- **Learning**: machine-certified running times for the LMN and Kushilevitz–Mansour learners; distributional problems and reductions, samplability, average-to-worst-case connections, PAC/agnostic learning, learning lower bounds, compression and refutation.
  Develop low-degree likelihood-ratio and sum-of-squares comparisons where justified, distinguishing restricted-model bounds from unrestricted hardness.
  Use the [distributional framework](complexity.md#average-case-complexity).
- **Realizable PAC learning** ([#2868](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2868)): the VC ε-net upper bound `O((d log (1/ε) + log (1/δ))/ε)` for general well-behaved classes over measurable domains, beyond countable domains.
- **Fundamental theorem of statistical learning** ([#4912](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4912)): for binary classes, the equivalence of uniform convergence, agnostic and realizable PAC learnability, learnability by ERM and finite VC dimension (Shalev-Shwartz–Ben-David, Theorem 6.7) beyond countable domains.
  Also the lower halves of their Theorem 6.8: the agnostic `Ω((d + log (1/δ))/ε²)` bound and the `log (1/δ)/ε` term of the realizable bound.
- **Agnostic learning of infinite classes on uncountable domains** ([#3005](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3005)): agnostic learnability of finite VC classes at the sharp `O((d + log (1/δ))/ε²)` rate on general measurable domains, under a measurability hypothesis on the class.
- **Nonuniform learning** ([#4915](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4915)): the characterization of nonuniformly learnable classes as countable unions of finite-VC classes, and consistency.
- **Sample compression** ([#4914](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4914)): unlabelled compression schemes.
- **Boosting** ([#4913](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4913)): generalization of the boosted class beyond finite domains, through the VC dimension of weighted majorities or the convex-hull margin bound, and weak-to-strong PAC learnability.
- **PAC-Bayes** ([#4916](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4916)): the Maurer–Seeger `kl` form on general measurable hypothesis spaces and with Maurer's `2√m` constant, and PAC-Bayes margin bounds for linear predictors.
- **Rademacher complexity** ([#3297](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3297)): the population generalization, agnostic ERM and margin bounds on general measurable domains rather than finite ones.
  Also local Rademacher complexity.
- **Linear predictors** ([#4919](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4919)): kernel classes.
- **Stability and regularization** ([#4917](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4917)): high-probability stability bounds (Bousquet–Elisseeff, Feldman–Vondrák) and the stability of SGD.
- **Online-to-batch conversion** ([#4918](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4918)): a high-probability SGD bound, `log T / T` rates for strongly convex losses and running-time bounds.
- **Multiclass and real-valued learning** ([#5084](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/5084)): the lower bounds of the multiclass fundamental theorem (Shalev-Shwartz–Ben-David, Theorem 29.3) and its extension beyond countable domains; pseudo-dimension and fat-shattering dimension.
- **Cryptographic hardness of learning** ([#4927](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4927)): the uniform form, in which polynomially secure generators rule out polynomial-time learners for polynomial-size circuits, even with membership queries.
  Also the Kearns–Valiant, Kharitonov and Klivans–Sherstov hardness results, learning algorithms implying circuit lower bounds, and average-case hardness of learning versus one-way functions.
- **Statistical query learning** ([#2910](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2910)): simulation of SQ learners under random classification noise of known and unknown rate (Kearns's Lemma 2 and Theorem 3), the single-sample form of Kearns's Theorem 1 and a distribution-free variant, and randomized SQ learners.
- **Statistical query dimensions** ([#2910](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2910)): the Bshouty–Feldman transfer to correlational queries, the worst-case SQ dimension versus VC dimension (Reyzin's Observations 20 and 21), Feldman's strong SQ dimension, and his statistical dimension for a planted problem with matching sample and runtime upper bounds.
- **Online learning in the mistake-bound model** ([#2908](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2908)): WINNOW2 and attribute transformations (Littlestone 1988, Theorems 9 and 10), and optimal `Θ(√(Ldim(C) T))` agnostic regret under adaptive adversaries and unknown horizons.
  Also real-valued analogues and running-time bounds for the standard optimal algorithm.
- **Monotonicity testing**: the `Ω(√n)` lower bound for non-adaptive one-sided testers (Fischer et al. 2002), larger ranges and hypergrids, and the directed-isoperimetry path testers with `Õ(√n / ε²)` queries (Khot–Minzer–Safra).
- **Distribution testing**: the matching `Ω(n^{2/3} ε^{-4/3} + √n ε^{-2})` sample lower bound for closeness testing, and the `Ω(√n / ε²)` uniformity lower bound on domains of odd size.
  Also instance-optimal identity testing, tolerant and structured-property testing, further access models, and further sample lower bounds.
- **Dense graph property testing**: `H`-freeness for general `H` through removal lemmas, sharper bipartiteness bounds, `k`-colourability with `poly(1/ε)` queries, and the two-sided case of the Goldreich–Trevisan canonical-tester theorem.
  Testers should decide by the property itself when it is closed under induced subgraphs (Proposition D.2, due to Alon) or is a graph partition property (Corollary 5.9).
- **Liu–Pass in the near-linear regime** ([#798](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/798)): Liu–Pass Theorem 3.1 (c) in the near-linear regime, hardness for every polynomial `t(n) ≥ (1 + ε)n`, which needs a rate-1 efficient conditional EP-PRG running in `n + O(n^c)` multi-tape steps for a constant `c < 1`.
- **Partial-MCSP hardness refinements** ([#798](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/798)): Hirahara's `(log N)^ε` inapproximability and distributional forms of partial-MCSP hardness, `NC¹-MCSP*`, `MKTP*`, and NP-hardness of learning programs (FOCS'22 Thm I.1).
  Also the `(log N)^ε` inapproximability of `MINKT*`, Liu–Pass's RAM `McK^tP`, ACMTV's `McKTP` and Hirahara's `MINcKT^SAT`.
- **Further metacomplexity developments** ([#798](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/798)): the reverse multi-tape-to-single-tape simulation and machine-backed Nisan–Wigderson decoding.
  Also, for Multi-output MCSP, Ilango–Loff–Oliveira's MDCP hardness (their Section 3), search-to-decision reduction (their Section 4.3) and communication minimization problems (their Section 5).
- **Learning from natural proofs** ([#798](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/798)): for Carmosino–Impagliazzo–Kabanets–Kolokolova's learner from a natural property useful against a class, Theorem 5.1 in abstract form ([#4635](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4635)), odd `p` (GRS decoding over GF(p), the von Neumann step, Smolensky's dimension property), a machine-level runtime certificate (`IsOraclePPTOn`), compression (Corollaries 5.2 and 5.5) and the MCSP search-to-decision consequence.
- **Chaitin's incompleteness theorem** ([#6859](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/6859)): Chaitin's incompleteness theorem for the multi-tape plain complexity (which needs compiling partial recursive functions to programs of the universal Turing machine), Solovay's refinements, and the Kritchman–Raz proof of Gödel's second incompleteness theorem.
- **Hardness magnification and the locality barrier** ([#798](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/798)): the locality barrier of Chen–Hirahara–Oliveira–Pich–Rajgopal–Santhanam (Theorem 59 and Corollary 61, ECCC numbering), which needs exponent-2 shrinkage under `k`-wise independent restrictions and Hirahara–Santhanam's `N^{2−o(1)}` formula lower bound for MCSP; the remaining bullets of their Proposition 63 (`[AK10]`, `[LW13]`, `[OS18]` `k`-VC and `[OPS19]` Gap-MKtP); and their Theorem 1 linking natural proofs, learning and pseudorandom functions.
- **Symmetry of information** ([#798](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/798)): symmetry of information for the binary-string models and for time-bounded complexity, with its connection to one-way functions (Liu–Pass, Hirahara).
- **Quantum computation**: channel semantics and approximation costs alongside circuit and measurement semantics, reusing suitable quantum foundations rather than incompatible models; a query-to-circuit bridge; complete problems for BQP; related query, communication and interactive classes with amplification, containments and complete problems.
  Supply the [quantum adversary models](cryptography.md#concurrent-and-quantum-security) needed by cryptographic reductions ([#4987](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4987)).
- **Gate-set universality** ([#3350](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3350)): `BQP` over arbitrary elementary gates equals `BQP` over Hadamard, NOT, CNOT and Toffoli, through Shi's constructive approximation of real rotations (Lemma 4.5, Proposition 4.2 and Theorem 4.6), the exact decomposition of real orthogonal gates into two-level rotations, and polynomial-time uniform synthesis.
  Also Kitaev's universality of `{Λ(P(i)), H}` and the Solovay–Kitaev theorem.
- **QMA** ([#2876](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2876)): error reduction from an inverse-polynomial gap, strong error reduction that keeps the witness length, mixed witnesses, QCMA and the local Hamiltonian problem.
- **Quantum information primitives** ([#3329](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3329)): no-broadcasting for mixed states under CPTP maps.
- **Quantum information bounds** ([#3329](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3329)): Nayak's bound for quantum random access codes over von Neumann entropy and mutual information.
- **Quantum query separations** ([#2991](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2991)): polynomial-time post-processing of Simon's algorithm by Gaussian elimination over `ZMod 2`, separations in the value-query model, and then Forrelation and other optimal quantum–classical query separations.
- **Promise problems in quantum query complexity**: exact quantum lower bounds for promise problems via polynomial degree (`Q_E ≥ deg/2` on the promise domain); a promise form of `randomizedComplexity`; and recursive Fourier sampling (Bernstein–Vazirani).
- **Quantum query lower bounds** ([#3239](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3239)): comparison-tree, approximate-mean and relative-counting variants of Nayak–Wu.
- **Shor's algorithm**: bounded-error polynomial-time integer factoring and discrete logarithms in the native uniform quantum-circuit model, with explicit encodings, success probabilities and gate-approximation costs.
  It needs circuits for the quantum Fourier transform, period finding for `r ∤ N` by continued fractions and the `Ω(1 / log log r)` totient bound, phase estimation, reversible modular exponentiation and the classical reductions, certifying uniform generation and polynomial gate bounds.
- **Nonlocal games** ([#6149](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/6149)): the Grothendieck-constant bound on quantum advantage in XOR games (Cleve–Høyer–Toner–Watrous Theorem 7); parallel repetition of entangled games; isometric self-testing of the CHSH strategy; and the class `MIP*`, toward the optional [MIP* = RE](../ROADMAP.md#optional-landmark-projects) project.

### Differential privacy and adaptive data analysis

- **Differential privacy mechanisms**: privacy and utility proofs for mechanisms with sampling cost and finite-precision error, covering exact sampling of discrete noise and continuous Laplace and Gaussian mechanisms.
  Also concentrated privacy accounting.
- **Private query answering and learning** ([#2977](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2977)): private query answering and private learning, building on the exponential mechanism and the sparse vector technique; privacy–accuracy and sample-complexity lower bounds; then adaptive data-analysis and generalization guarantees.
  Keep the adjacency relation, interaction model, privacy parameters and computational resources explicit, reusing the probability, oracle-computation and learning APIs.
- **Privacy–accuracy lower bounds** ([#3201](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3201)): settling whether Dwork–Roth's constant `(2 α / η) ^ 2` in Theorem 8.2 holds for `η / 2 ≤ α < 1 / 2`, Theorem 8.8 for add/remove adjacency on histograms, and `(ε, δ)` lower bounds via fingerprinting codes.
